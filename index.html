Welcome to GreenStep Foundation

We help communities in South Africa live sustainably through education, clean-ups, tree planting and recycling programmes.

What We Do

Community Clean-Ups

We organise monthly clean-ups in parks, streets and rivers to keep our neighbourhoods clean and safe.

Tree Planting

We plant indigenous trees at schools and community centres to fight climate change and create shade.

Recycling Education

We teach schools and families how to sort waste, recycle and reduce plastic use.
