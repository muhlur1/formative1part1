About Us

GreenStep Foundation is a non-profit organisation based in Wes-Rand, Gauteng. We started in 2022 with a small group of volunteers cleaning up a local park. Today we work with schools, businesses and community groups across Gauteng.

Our mission is to make sustainable living easy and practical for every family. Our vision is a clean, green South Africa where communities take care of their environment.

Our Values

Community First

We work with communities, not just for them. Every project is led by local people.

Sustainability

We focus on long-term change, not once-off events. We teach skills that last.

Education

We believe knowledge changes behaviour. We run workshops for children and adults.

Our Team

Our team is made up of volunteers, teachers, environmentalists and youth leaders. We are passionate about a greener future for South Africa.
