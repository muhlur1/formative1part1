Our Services

We offer practical programmes to help schools, businesses and communities live more sustainably.

School Workshops

Interactive lessons on recycling, water saving and climate change for Grade R to Grade 12 learners.

Price: Free for public schools

Community Clean-Ups

We organise and supply equipment for clean-ups. We also help communities start their own clean-up groups.

Price: Free - sponsored by partners

Tree Planting Days

We supply indigenous trees and teach communities how to plant and care for them.

Price: R50 per tree, includes planting kit

Recycling Stations

We help schools and businesses set up sorting stations for paper, plastic, glass and cans.

Price: From R1,500 per station
